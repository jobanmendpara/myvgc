# MyVGC: domain context

MyVGC is a hub where a competitive player tracks their Pokémon VGC experience in the game **Pokémon Champions**: tournaments attended, the teams they brought, who they played, what those opponents brought, and notes on all of it. Teams can be exported to the official VGC teamsheet format.

## Glossary

- **VGC**: Video Game Championships, the official Pokémon competitive format. In Champions it is doubles: each player brings a Team of 6 and picks 4 of them for each Game.
- **Regulation**: the ruleset in force for a period, such as Reg M-C. It decides which Pokémon, items and mechanics are legal. Each Regulation has a start and end date; the **current Regulation** is the one in force today. A new Tournament defaults to the Regulation in force on its date.
- **Legal / illegal**: a Team is legal in a Regulation when it breaks none of that Regulation's rules (allowed Pokémon, abilities and items, moves the species can learn, banned categories, Species Clause, Item Clause), and illegal when it breaks at least one. An illegal Team can still be saved; MyVGC warns, it doesn't block. Only the User's own Teams are judged: a Team against its own Regulation, and a Tournament's Team snapshot against the Tournament's Regulation. Opponents' Teams never are. A Regulation MyVGC has no rules for (such as M-A) can't be judged: its Teams are neither legal nor illegal, just unchecked.
- **Season**: the Play! Pokémon competitive year, running from the day after one Worlds to the end of the next, for example the 2027 Season. Championship Points reset each Season. A Tournament belongs to the Season its start date falls in. Seasons and their dates come with the app; Users don't define them. In MyVGC the 2026 Season starts on Champions' release (2026-04-08), so it only holds the part of that Season played in Champions.
- **User**: someone with a MyVGC account. Each User has a private space; nothing is shared between Users.
- **Player details**: what a User is known by in Play! Pokémon and in the game, written onto their Teamsheets: Player Name, Trainer Name in Game, Switch Profile Name, Player ID, Support ID (shown on the Pokémon Champions title screen), Date of Birth and Age Division. All optional. With a Date of Birth, Age Division is worked out for each Season instead of being set by hand.
- **Account settings**: settings kept on the User on the server, so they follow the User to every device. Player details are Account settings. Edited on the Settings page.
- **Device settings**: settings kept in one browser, such as the theme. They differ from device to device, survive logging out and work offline. Edited on the Settings page.
- **List style**: how the list of Tournaments, of Teams or of Opponents is shown: **Compact** (one line per item), **Detailed** (several lines, close to a card) or **Grouped** (under headings, such as Tournaments by year or Teams by Regulation). Each of the three lists has its own List style, and it is an Account setting. It is changed at the top of the list it styles, not on the Settings page, and is not part of the Data export.
- **Team**: up to six Pokémon builds. One type covers both the User's own teams (fully known) and an Opponent's Team (often partly known). Everything except species is optional.
- **Pokémon build**: one Pokémon on a Team. In Champions it has a species or form, ability, held item, up to four different moves, a Stat Alignment and Stat Points (0 to 32 per stat, 66 in total). Champions has no EVs, IVs to set, Tera or level to choose; level is always 50.
- **Stat Alignment**: what Champions calls a nature, such as Adamant: it raises one stat and lowers another. Showdown pastes call it Nature; MyVGC says Stat Alignment.
- **Stat value**: a Pokémon build's final stat at level 50, as the game shows it, such as 169 Speed. It is worked out from the species' base stats, its Stat Points and its Stat Alignment, and never stored. Not to be confused with Stat Points.
- **Opponent's Team**: the Team an Opponent brought to a Tournament, recorded once per Opponent per Tournament and shared by every Round against them there. It only exists for an Opponent the User played in that Tournament, and it is not one of the User's Teams. An Opponent's page shows the one they brought to each Tournament, newest first. It holds what an open teamsheet shows (species, ability, held item, moves), is usually entered by choosing species, and may be partly known: from one to six species. Never a Team snapshot: there is no original to copy.
- **Opponent**: a private record a User keeps about someone they played. Opponents are not Users. An Opponent has a display name plus Aliases.
- **Alias**: another name an Opponent goes by, such as a legal name, screen name, Discord username or nickname. Opponent search matches display names and Aliases.
- **Merge**: combining a duplicate Opponent into another, the survivor, when both are the same person. The duplicate's Rounds and Note entries move to the survivor, its display name and Aliases become the survivor's Aliases, its notes are added after the survivor's, and it is deleted. A merge can't be undone.
- **Tournament**: a competitive event the User took part in. It records a name, date(s), optional location, online or in person, an Event tier, a Regulation, an optional Organizer, an optional Entry fee, optional Championship Points gained, an optional player count, an optional external link (such as RK9 or Limitless), a default best-of (1 or 3) for its Rounds, a Placement, and whether the User Dropped. It has at most one Team snapshot of the User's Team, one Teamsheet, and is made up of Rounds. MyVGC only records play inside Tournaments; ladder and practice games are not tracked.
- **Record**: a Tournament's wins, losses and ties, always worked out from its Rounds and never entered by hand. Swiss and Top cut are shown separately (for example 6-2-0, then 0-1 in Top cut). A Bye counts as a win in the Record, but not in win rates or head-to-head records.
- **Top cut stage**: what a Top cut Round is called, by the number of players left: Top 64, Top 32, Top 16, Top 8, Top 4, Finals. Losing in a stage suggests the Placement (losing in Top 4 suggests 3rd).
- **Upcoming Tournament**: a Tournament whose start date is still in the future. It has no Rounds yet, but its Entry fee counts toward spending.
- **Head-to-head record**: the User's wins, losses and ties in Rounds against one Opponent, leaving out Byes.
- **Placement**: where the User finished in a Tournament (for example 9th of 128), entered by hand. Winning a Tournament means a Placement of 1st.
- **Dropped**: the User left a Tournament before it ended. A Dropped Tournament usually has no Placement.
- **Organizer**: who ran a Tournament, such as a local game store or community. Just a name, not a record of its own.
- **Entry fee**: what the User paid to enter a Tournament: an amount and a currency. Totals across different currencies are never converted into one another.
- **Championship Points (CP)**: the Play! Pokémon points a User earned at a Tournament, entered by hand. MyVGC does not calculate them. Because CP resets each Season, CP is only ever totalled within one Season.
- **Event tier**: the kind of Tournament, from a fixed list: Premier Challenge, Regional, International, Worlds, Grassroots, Online, Other.
- **Teamsheet**: the official Play! Pokémon team list for a Tournament. It can be generated in the app from a Team, as a PDF whose fields the User can still edit, or uploaded as a PDF (a Teamsheet attachment).
- **Teamsheet attachment**: a Teamsheet PDF the User uploaded to a Tournament, kept exactly as uploaded for reference. At most one per Tournament; uploading again replaces it. Not part of the Data export.
- **Reading a Team**: turning a file, such as a Teamsheet PDF, into a draft Team that the User reviews before saving. Nothing is saved and the file isn't kept. Called reading, never importing: importing means pastes and the Data export.
- **Replica Team**: Champions' in-game way of sharing a Battle Team, which judges may ask to see.
- **Replica Team ID**: the code that identifies a Replica Team, such as `9TCG84KWXK`. Players often write it in a Teamsheet's "Battle Team Number / Name" box.
- **Round**: one pairing in a Tournament. Never called "Match". It has a phase (Swiss or Top cut) and, within it, a round number (Swiss) or a Top cut stage, is best of 1 or best of 3 (defaulting to the Tournament's setting), and has a result, an Opponent, notes and, optionally, its Games. Every Round except a Bye has an Opponent.
- **Round result**: Win, Loss or Tie. Intentional draws and time-outs count as Ties.
- **Bye**: a Round with no Opponent, which counts as a win.
- **Game score**: how many Games each side won in a best-of-3 Round, such as 2-1. It is optional; when it is given, it decides the Round result. Game results fill it in once they decide the Round.
- **Game**: a single battle within a Round, numbered 1 to 3. A best-of-1 Round has at most one, a best-of-3 Round at most three, and a Bye none. A Game records each side's Brought Pokémon and Leads, a Game result and notes, all optional, and a Round can record Game 3 without Game 2.
- **Game result**: Win, Loss or Tie for one Game. In best of 3, Game results fill in the Game score once a side has won 2 or Game 3 is recorded, with no Game before it left blank; in best of 1, Game 1's result is the Round's. They never contradict a Game score or result the User gave.
- **Brought Pokémon**: the up to four Pokémon a side picks from its Team for one Game. Usually chosen from the Tournament's Team snapshot (the User's side) or the Opponent's Team (the Opponent's side), but any species can be recorded.
- **Lead**: one of the up to two Brought Pokémon a side sends out first in a Game.
- **Lead pair**: the unordered pair of a side's two Leads in a Game, so {Incineroar, Amoonguss} is the same as {Amoonguss, Incineroar}. A Game with one Lead recorded has no Lead pair.
- **Team snapshot**: a frozen copy of the User's Team attached to a Tournament. It links back to the original Team.
- **Notes**: one Markdown notes field on each Opponent, Team, Tournament and Round. On an Opponent it is a standing summary; dated observations go in Note entries. An Opponent's page shows its Note entries and the notes from every Round against them in one timeline, newest first.
- **Note entry**: a dated note on an Opponent, such as what they brought or how they played on a given day. Its date is a calendar date the User picks, today by default. It stands alone, linked to no Tournament or Round.
- **Data export**: a file holding all of one User's data, which they can download and import into another install, hosted or self-hosted. It includes the User's Player details. Importing adds what the account doesn't have yet and never changes or deletes what it does. Account details such as email and password are never part of it.
- **Statistics**: the page showing what the recorded Opponent's Teams and Games say about how the User plays: their record against each species faced (counting Rounds), and by Lead pair, single Lead and Brought Pokémon (counting Games, the User's side only). Byes are always left out and Ties count as played but not won, as on the dashboard. Rounds or Games that can't be counted, such as a Round whose Opponent has no Opponent's Team or a Game with no Game result, are left out and each table says how many.
